Mashed Potato -

1kg (2.2lbs) - Kestrel, Russet or Yukon Gold Potatoes, Peeled & Halved
100g - Unsalted Butter, Softened
150ml - Full Fat Milk, Warmed
2 - Free Range Egg Yolks
4 Tbsp (30g) - Parmigiana Cheese (Parmesan)

Cottage Pie -

2 Tbsp - Olive Oil
2 eggs
Beef Mince - 1kg
Carrot - 1 Peeled & Grated
Onion - 1 Grated
Celery Rib Large 1 Grated
Garlic Cloves - 3 Grated
Parsley Flat Leaf 3g - Finely Chopped
Rosemary Leaves 3g Finely Chopped (optional)
Thyme Sprigs - 3 Leaves Only, Finely Chopped
Tomato Paste -2 Tbsp (50g)
Worcestershire Sauce - 2 Tbsp (30ml)
Red Wine 250ml (1 Cup) Vegetable or Chicken Stock
Beef/Vegetable/Chicken Stock - 250ml (1 Cup)
Frozen Peas - 1 Cup (Big cup or small cup?)
Seasoning to taste

Mashed potatoes:
Boil potatoes. Once cooked, pour the potatoes through a colander (sieve) and allow them to drain.
While potatoes are boilding, warm milk + Butter so they are warm *not* boiling
Mash potatoes *very well* and add warmed milk + Butter, add seasoning to taste.
Add in two eggs *after* seasoning to taste.

Meat:
Add olive oil and beef mince to hot pan.
Add salt & pepper and brown the beef.
Add onions, carrots, celery, garlic, flat-leaf parsley, rosemary, thyme, concentrated tomato paste, and Worcestershire sauce. Mix well and cook for 5 minutes.
Add the red wine and deglaze the pan if needed. Reduce for 5 minutes or until reduced by half.
Add beef/chicken/vegetable stock and frozen peas and bring to a boil.
Reduce heat to low and cook for 15 minutes.
Check regularly and adjust seasoning.
Remove from heat.

Combine:
Preheat oven to 180.c - 350.f.
Add the beef mix to a large roasting dish.
Add the mashed potatoes over the top and gently spread them out.
Use a fork to rough the potato surface and grate over the parmesan cheese.
Bake for 20-25 minutes or until the mashed potatoes are golden and crispy. Remove and rest for 10 minutes.

Gruyere Cheese - Grated into mash
Pre-heat oven to 375℉.
Bacon - Chopped
Mushrooms - chopped reasonably finely


Ingredients

Mashed Potato:

    2.5 lbs (1200g) russet potatoes, peeled cut into 1” cubes
    ½ cup (115g) butter
    ½ cup(120ml) whole milk
    ⅓ cup  (85g) creme fraiche
    ½ cup (75g) Gruyere cheese
    Kosher salt and pepper to taste

Filling:

    1.5 lbs ground beef or lamb (lamb is traditional)
    6 oz finely chopped or ground bacon
    1 Tbsp unsalted butter
    3 diced trumpet mushrooms
    1 medium yellow onion, diced
    1 celery rib, diced
    1 large carrot, diced
    4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
    2 Tbsp (30g) tomato paste
    ½ cup (120 ml) red wine, for deglazing
    2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 Tbsp (5g) fresh thyme, minced
    1 Tbsp (6g) fresh rosemary
    1 cup (240 ml) beef stock
    1 cup 140g frozen peas
    ½ cup (75g) Parmigiano Reggiano, grated
    ½ cup (75g) Gruyere, grated for topping



    Directions

Mashed Potato:

    For the mashed potato toppings, rinse and peel the russet potatoes and cut lengthwise then lengthwise again and then into quarters.
    Dump all potatoes into a medium pot and cover with water. Season generously with salt to taste. Set over medium-high heat.
    Bring to a boil and let boil for 7-10 minutes. Potatoes should be easily pierced with a fork. Drain once finished.
    In a small sauce pot, add in unsalted butter and whole milk. Set on the stove over medium heat just until the butter is melted and mixture is hot.
    Rice/mash all the potatoes using a potato ricer or potato masher. Add in the hot butter/milk mixture and mix together before stirring in creme fraiche.
    Grate in gruyere cheese. Season to taste with salt and pepper and mix together until thoroughly combined.

Filling:

    Pre-heat oven to 375℉.
    If not using store bought ground beef, start by slicing the beef (or lamb) and thoroughly mix with roughly chopped bacon.
    In a stand mixer fitted with a meat grinder attachment, grind the beef and bacon mixture. Once finished, knead into a firm dough like ball.
    Heat a medium size pot (or pan) over medium-high heat until smoking hot. No oil included.
    Add in the beef, pressing it in and sear for 3 minutes. Flip and repeat on the other side.
    When both sides are seared. Mash the beef until fine, season with salt and pepper, and continue cooking over medium heat in its own fat until evenly brown.
    Dump out all the beef into a separate bowl and place to the side.
    Add butter to the pot (or pan) and melt over medium heat.
    Once bubbling, add in mushrooms and sear until just brown before adding in the onions, celery, carrots, and garlic. Season with salt and pepper. Saute for 2 - 3 minutes mixing occasionally.
    When softened, toss in tomato paste sauteing for 2 minutes or until caramelized. Deglaze with red wine, stock, or vinegar.
    Follow with Worcestershire sauce and bring to a boil. Add in fresh chopped thyme, fresh chopped rosemary, mix to combine and let reduce until all the liquid is gone.
    Add in beef stock seasoning to taste with salt and pepper. Mix and let cook for 1-2 minutes.
    Add in the frozen peas and stir together. Let cook until the peas are nice and hot.
    Dump the meat and vegetable mixture into a casserole dish spreading out evenly to the edges of the pan. Press the meat down so it’s nice and compact.
    Gently add the mashed potatoes on top and evenly spread across the surface of the casserole.
    Grate on some Parmigiano Reggiano and Gruyere cheese. Optionally fork the top of the surface.
    Place into a 375℉ oven for 20 minutes or until top is golden brown.
    Let sit for 20 minutes before serving. Optionally garnish with thinly sliced chives.
